This directory contains the materials for Phase 3,the Three-Condition Experiment for generating SDBs. 

<img width="1624" height="675" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72b9b183-8094-429e-bf88-991487bb7e1b" />

In Phase 3, identical, unified experimental prompts to generate the SDB are executed under a comparative three condition design.Three experimental conditions are constructed to isolate the contribution of contextual retrieval and multi-notebook synthesis. The foundational LLM, generation prompts, and evaluation procedures are held strictly constant across all conditions. This design isolates retrieval architecture as the sole manipulated variable.

<img width="1285" height="717" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/714f9853-6c2a-469c-a774-bbbfd8d6261e" />

Uniform SDB generation prompt. A single uniform generation prompt was developed to produce sociocultural data biographies (SDBs) under each experimental condition (Scenarios 1 – 3). The prompt functions as a controlled research instrument and remains constant throughout formal data collection.
