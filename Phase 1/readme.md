Background:
Original data scraped from the Legacy of Slavery project at the Maryland State Archives (https://slavery.msa.maryland.gov/).  

Primary Corpus: Sample of 50 records from the scraped dataset. The primary corpus is treated as a fixed evidentiary dataset for purposes of the experiment. The same fifty records are used without substitution in all three experimental conditions. 

Notebook 2 materials were curated by the researcher and instantiated within a dedicated NotebookLM notebook, separate from the primary corpus in Notebook 1.

•	Archival Guides and Historical Datasets
•	The Economics and Accounting of Commoditization
•	Legal and Regulatory Frameworks
•	Regional Agricultural Shifts and Labor Markets
